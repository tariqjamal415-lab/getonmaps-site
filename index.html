FAQ

Common questions

Do you guarantee I'll rank #1 on Google Maps?

No. Nobody controls Google's rankings, and anyone who guarantees a position, a number of reviews or a number of leads isn't being straight with you. What we do is make your profile complete, accurate and easy to act on — so when people find you, you look legit and they can call in one tap.

Do you write, buy or post reviews?

Never. Fake or incentivized reviews break Google's rules and can get a profile suspended. We give you a simple, compliant way to ask real customers for a review — a short text/email script plus a direct link and QR code.

How long does it take?

About 5–7 days from kickoff for the standard pack. If your profile needs verification or has ownership issues, Google's own processes can add time — we'll tell you up front if that's the case.

What does it cost?

The standard pack is a flat $1,200. Depending on how much cleanup your listing needs, scoped work can land between $750 and $2,000 — you'll see the exact price in writing before anything starts. No monthly retainer is required.

Do I need to give you my Google password?

No. You stay the owner of your Business Profile and everything we build. We'll agree on the simplest way to get the access we need on the free review call, and the 15-minute handoff shows you how to run it yourself afterward.

I already have a website. Is this still useful?

Often, yes. We make your Google profile and reviews match your site. If your current site is slow or hard to use on a phone, the one-page site gives people a fast page with a big call button — you decide which one your profile links to.

I already pay someone for SEO.

That's fine — we don't replace them. The pack covers the practical pieces a lot of SEO work skips: a clean profile, a review-ask routine and a simple page built to get calls.

What kinds of businesses is this for?

Any local business that gets customers from Google Maps: home services like cleaning, plumbing, HVAC, roofing, landscaping, pest control and electrical; salons, barbers and med spas; dentists and chiropractors; auto shops; restaurants and cafes; and local professional services. If you have a phone number and a real service area or location, you're likely a fit.

What happens after the handoff?

You own everything: the profile, the page and the review-ask templates. The 15-minute handoff call shows you how to keep it updated yourself. If you later want a fuller site, there's an optional Website in a Week upgrade.
